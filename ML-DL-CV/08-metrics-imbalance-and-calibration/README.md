# Metrics Imbalance and Calibration

[Back to ML-DL-CV](../README.md) · [Your practice space](practice/README.md)

## In simple words

Metrics translate predictions into task-specific quality measures.

## What to understand

Compare precision, recall, F1, PR/ROC curves, MAE/RMSE, calibration, and threshold costs.

## Think through this example

For a rare condition, predicting negative every time can have high accuracy and no useful recall.

## Common mistake

Choosing accuracy alone or interpreting every score as a calibrated probability.
