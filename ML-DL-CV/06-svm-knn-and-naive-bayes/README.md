## In simple words

These are useful classical alternatives to trees and neural networks.

## What to understand

SVM uses margins and possibly kernels; KNN compares neighbors; Naive Bayes uses conditional independence assumptions.

## Think through this example

Sparse text can suit a simple probabilistic baseline before a larger model.

## Common mistake

Ignoring scaling for distance/margin methods or treating independence assumptions as facts.
