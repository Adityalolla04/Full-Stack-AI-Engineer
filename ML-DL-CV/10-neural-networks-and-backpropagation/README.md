## In simple words

Neural networks compose parameterized layers and learn through gradients.

## What to understand

Understand forward computation, loss, chain rule, batching, parameter updates, and tensor shapes.

## Think through this example

A classification network's final layer and loss must agree on labels and output interpretation.

## Common mistake

Mixing incompatible output activations and losses.
