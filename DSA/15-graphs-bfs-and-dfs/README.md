# Graphs BFS and DFS

[Back to DSA](../README.md) · [Your practice space](practice/README.md)

## In simple words

Graphs model arbitrary relationships; BFS explores layers and DFS follows branches.

## What to understand

Track visited nodes to handle cycles. BFS gives shortest edge-count distances in unweighted graphs; DFS does not generally do so.

## Think through this example

For prerequisites A to B to C, BFS from A reaches C in two edges.

## Common mistake

Revisiting cycles forever or forgetting disconnected components.

## Practice yourself

Count connected components and find an unweighted shortest path; compare adjacency lists with matrices.
