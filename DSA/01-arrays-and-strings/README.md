# Arrays and Strings

[Back to DSA](../README.md) · [Your practice space](practice/README.md)

## In simple words

Arrays hold ordered elements; strings hold characters and are immutable in Python.

## What to understand

Compare indexing, traversal, insertion, slicing, and copying. List indexing is O(1); scanning and middle insertion are O(n).

## Think through this example

For study minutes 10, 20, 30, the second entry is 20 and the total is 60.

## Common mistake

Confusing a copied slice with a view, or repeatedly concatenating large strings.
