# Chunking Strategies

[Back to LLM](../README.md) · [Your practice space](practice/README.md)

## In simple words

Chunking divides documents into retrievable pieces.

## What to understand

Compare fixed-size, overlapping, section-based, and semantic chunks. Preserve source IDs and location metadata.

## Think through this example

A heading and its following paragraph may need to stay together to preserve meaning.

## Common mistake

Optimizing chunk size without evaluating retrieval on real questions.

## Practice yourself

Compare two chunking plans for a manual, including tables and section boundaries.
