## In simple words

Probabilistic language models assign likelihoods; sequence networks carry information across ordered inputs.

## What to understand

Review n-grams, smoothing, Markov assumptions, RNNs, LSTMs, and GRUs before attention.

## Think through this example

A bigram model uses much less context than a recurrent model, but is easier to inspect.

## Common mistake

Confusing high sequence likelihood with factual truth.
